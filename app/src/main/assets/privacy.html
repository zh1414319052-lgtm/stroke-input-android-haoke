Privacy Policy

Haoke edition · 2026-10-29

Offline input

Typing, handwriting and voice recognition are processed on this device. Microphone permission is requested only when you choose voice input. The recognition model is bundled in the APK. Each recording lasts at most 30 seconds and stays in memory: recordings are not saved or uploaded. Typed text, quick phrases, handwriting and recognition results are never sent to the update service. Recognised text is inserted into your current text field; leaving that input session or cancelling discards the recording. Microphone access can be revoked in Android settings.

Version checks

Internet access is used only to request the fixed HTTPS Haoke signed-update feed. Checks run on the foreground settings screen when you are not editing or using a keyboard or other dialog. You can also tap “Check for Haoke updates”. Leaving the screen cancels the request. The input method does not check updates while you type, handwrite or record.

There is no account, analytics, device identifier, background polling, automatic download or automatic installation. ONNX Runtime telemetry is disabled. Normal connection metadata, including your IP address, is necessarily visible to the website.

Your update choices

Version integrity checkpoints and reminder choices stay in this app's private settings. “Later” allows another reminder after 24 hours; “Ignore this version” suppresses that version. Manual checks can show it again. Release information must pass signature and app-identity checks before display.

Only tapping “View version” opens the version page in your external browser. That browser and any download website then apply their own privacy policies. Bundled privacy, help and about pages do not load remote resources. Other website and source-code links also open only when you tap them.
